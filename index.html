Bladwijzers – Kaartenmagazijn Topografische Dienst

IIIF manifests and JSON metadata for 4,558 digitised map sheets (Leiden University Libraries & National Library of Australia), linked to the bladwijzers of the Kaartenmagazijn of the Topografische Dienst, Dutch East Indies. Everything: IIIF collection · all_maps.json · all_sheets.csv · source & README.

“Bekijk” opens the sheets in the built-in viewer. The IIIF collections also work in Mirador or Universal Viewer, but sheets from the National Library of Australia show only in the built-in viewer: NLA does not allow its images to be loaded by other websites. NLA also asks every visitor to pass a short bot check once on nla.gov.au; if an NLA sheet does not appear, the viewer links you there.
